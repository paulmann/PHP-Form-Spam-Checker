# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully and give it the same way.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Show empathy towards other community members.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, including sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing another person's private information, such as a physical or
  e-mail address, without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, its issue tracker, pull requests, the wiki
and any other space where someone represents this project. It also applies when
an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour through a private
channel: open a GitHub Security Advisory on the repository (Security → Report a
vulnerability) or contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile. Reports are
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly; the privacy and safety of the
reporter are respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any
contributor for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or
harmful. Enforcement decisions are explained to the person concerned where that
is safe for everyone involved.
